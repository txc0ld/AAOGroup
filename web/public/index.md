## Why it hits local business hardest

Your business lives on local discovery: Google, Maps, word of mouth. Now AI gives the answer at the top of the page, and people ask it for a name instead of scrolling the listings. Being findable isn't enough anymore — you have to be the one it recommends.

## How the AI picks who it picks

It trusts whatever is clearest. Here's what we fix:

1. **Google Business Profile, machine-perfect.** Half-finished profiles are why AI skips you. Every service listed, exact suburbs, right categories, real hours, real photos.
2. **Plain-text services, prices and area.** AI can't read a price inside a JPG or PDF. Clear words it can pick up and quote back.
3. **Reviews: plenty, recent, replied-to.** 12 reviews from 2022 reads as closed down. An easy way to ask every happy customer, and reply to all.
4. **Answer fast.** Slow callbacks teach AI to stop sending leads. Catch missed calls and reply in minutes.

## Common questions

### What's SEO, in plain English?

SEO is Search Engine Optimisation. It's the old game of getting your business to show up when someone Googles a local business, then letting them pick from the list. Still worth doing, but it's not the whole story anymore.

### And what's AEO?

AEO is Answer Engine Optimisation. Same idea, but for AI. Instead of getting you onto a list, it's about getting ChatGPT and Google's AI to actually name you when someone asks for a good local business. The AI only picks two or three, so being one it names is the new goal.

No. SEO put you on a list and let the customer choose. AI doesn't show a list, it names two or three businesses. The job is being one it trusts enough to name.
